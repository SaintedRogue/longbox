Lethal killer Cletus Kasady casts his symbiotic tendrils across the Marvel Universe in the blockbuster epic ABSOLUTE CARNAGE!

The psychopathic Carnage is targeting everyone who has ever worn a symbiote — and that’s a lot of potential victims, even for him! Naturally, Kasady’s new reign of terror spells misery for Spider-Man and Venom — but the devastation is felt by Miles Morales, the Avengers, Deadpool, Ghost Rider, the Immortal Hulk and more! But what is Kasady’s horrific endgame? Experience a new level of crazy as Carnage runs wild!
