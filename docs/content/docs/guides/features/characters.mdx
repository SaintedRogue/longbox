---
title: Characters
---

Browsing by character asks a question a folder tree cannot answer: _what do I own with
Venom in it?_ Longbox reads the characters recorded on each book and gives you that
list.

## Finding it

**Characters** in the sidebar.

## Where the list comes from

Characters are not something you configure. They are read from the **characters** field
on each book's metadata, and only books that have it contribute. A library whose books
carry no character metadata shows an empty page — the feature is working, there is just
nothing recorded for it to read.

## Getting characters onto your books

Two ways, and they stack:

- **Embedded metadata.** `ComicInfo.xml` inside a `.cbz` has a `Characters` field, and
  the scanner reads it. Files tagged by ComicTagger or similar usually have it already.
- **A metadata provider.** Matching a book fills in characters along with everything
  else — see [metadata fetching](/docs/guides/integrations/metadata-fetching). Marvel and
  DC books in particular tend to have rich character lists upstream.

<Callout type="info">
	**HOUSE RULES** — Character names are text, not identities. `Spider-Man`, `Spider-Man (Peter
	Parker)` and `Peter Parker` are three different entries, because that is genuinely what your files
	say. Consistency comes from consistent metadata, so matching a run against one provider gives
	tidier results than mixing sources.
</Callout>

## Editing them

A book's characters are editable in the metadata editor like any other field, and an
edit you make by hand is protected from being overwritten by a later provider fetch.
